[h1]Patch notes: desync fixes[/h1]

[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tutorial missions are now solo while a co-op session is open. Their scripted scenes run outside the simulation and drifted apart between the two games. Your friend gets a notice explaining why.
[*]Stalls are detected: if your friend's game is ahead and yours has been waiting on their merc for 15 seconds with nothing to replay, the games resync on their own.
[*]The friend's ship clock follows the host's time, speed and flight timer, including travel and tutorial pauses.
[*]The friend's star map now mirrors the host's. The friend's copy no longer simulates its own ship; the host sends the campaign changes and departures.
[/list]

[h2]Diagnostics[/h2]
[list]
[*]More detail is written to Player.log when a mission starts and when the host's world is received. If you end up in your own copy of the level instead of the host's, please send both players' Player.log files.
[/list]

[b]Both players must update to this build.[/b] The network protocol changed (v8), and the host refuses a session if the mod versions don't match.
